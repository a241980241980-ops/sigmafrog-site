For macOS

Paste a Drive link. Get the files.

Sigmafrog downloads Google Drive files and folders straight to your Mac — no browser zipping, original folder structure kept, and every file checked after it lands. Built for editors, colorists and studios who receive footage by Drive link.

Privacy Policy

Requires a Google account. Sigmafrog only reads and downloads — it never changes your Drive.

The Sigmafrog window (file names blurred).

How it works

Sign in with Google

One-time sign-in in your browser. Sigmafrog asks for read-only access.

Add links

Paste links or drag them into the window. Shared files and folders are both supported.

Choose a folder

Files download in parallel to where you want them, in their original folders.

What it does

No zip files

Folders arrive as folders, not as split archives from the browser.

Browse your Drive

Sign in to browse My Drive, Shared with me, shared drives, Recent and Starred, then download with one click.

Many links, one queue

Manage every download in one list: pause, resume, retry, open in Finder.

Checked on arrival

Each file is compared with Google Drive's size and checksum, so a broken or missing clip shows up now, not in the edit.

Simple by design

One window. Sensible defaults. Nothing to configure before your first download.

Your data stays on your Mac

  • Sigmafrog uses the drive.readonly permission only to browse your Drive, list and download the files you choose or add by link, and to check them after download.
  • It has no server of its own. Files, encrypted sign-in tokens and download history stay on your Mac; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no AI training on your files.

Sigmafrog's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
